Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Last updated / 最后更新: 2026-10-24 · changes

Cryptid Drop is a free, ad-supported puzzle game. It has no accounts, no sign-in, and no chat, and it never asks for your name, email, contacts, photos, or location. To see how the game is played, to fix crashes, and to show ads, it sends data to a small number of service providers. This page lists all of it.

This policy is provided by RAINWE GAMES ("we", "us"), the developer of Cryptid Drop: Blind Box Merge (the "Game").

1. What stays on your device

Your game progress (levels reached, stars, coins, boosters, collected figures) and your settings (sound, music) are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the Game deletes them. We do not run a server that stores your progress.

2. Anonymous gameplay analytics (PostHog)

Game analytics (GameAnalytics)

The Game also sends gameplay statistics to GameAnalytics (GameAnalytics Ltd.), a game analytics service, so we can see daily players, retention, level progress, and the in-game economy. It receives: session starts and lengths; level start, win, and fail with the score; in-game coin gains and spending; which ads were shown or rewarded; and purchases (item, price, and currency). With these it receives device information: device model, operating system and version, game version, connection type, language, IP address and the country derived from it, and a device identifier — on Android the advertising ID (GAID); on iOS the identifier for vendor (IDFV), and the IDFA only if you allowed tracking in Apple's prompt. GameAnalytics' privacy policy: gameanalytics.com/privacy

3. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

When the Game crashes or hits a script error, it sends a crash report to Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC) so we can fix the bug.

CategoryWhat is sent
Crash detailsStack trace and error message, whether the app was in the foreground, and the Game's recent log lines (for example "level start 12")
Game contextGame version and build, current screen, current level, game state
Device stateDevice model, operating system version, orientation, free memory and disk space
IdentifierA Firebase installation ID, a random ID unique to this install of the Game

Google retains Crashlytics crash data for up to 90 days. Firebase privacy information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

What we do not do: PostHog and Crashlytics never receive your advertising ID (IDFA / GAID); GameAnalytics does, as described above. The Game requests no device permissions such as camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or location. Advertising data, and Apple's tracking permission on iOS, are described in section 4.

4. Advertising and purchases

The Game shows a banner ad on the home screen, full-screen ads between levels, and optional rewarded video ads (for example "+5 drops"). Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies) and the ad networks it works with, currently ironSource Ads, Unity Ads, Google AdMob, and Meta Audience Network (we will update this list before adding another network). On iOS, the ironSource Ad Quality SDK also checks the ads that are shown, to detect broken, inappropriate, or fraudulent ads. To choose, show, cap, and measure ads, and to prevent ad fraud, these companies collect data from your device directly:

CategoryWhat is collected
Device identifiersOn Android, the Google advertising ID (GAID). On iOS, the identifier for vendor (IDFV), and the advertising ID (IDFA) only if you allow tracking (see below).
Device & networkDevice model, operating system and version, language, screen size, network type and carrier, IP address and the approximate location derived from it
Ad interactionsWhich ads were requested, shown, clicked, completed, or rewarded

On iOS, the Game shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt once before ads start. If you choose "Allow", the ad networks receive the IDFA and may use it to show ads based on your interests and to measure ads across apps; if you choose "Ask App not to Track", they do not receive it. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, these providers may use the advertising ID to show ads based on your interests. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). For California users, the Game turns on Meta's Limited Data Use option. Privacy policies of these providers:

  • Unity (LevelPlay, Unity Ads): unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Google AdMob: policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Meta Audience Network: facebook.com/privacy/policy

In-app purchases

The Game sells optional items: Remove Ads and the No Ads Bundle (one-time purchases), coin packs (Pile, Bag, Sack, and Chest of Coins), the Starter Pack, and the Rescue Pack. Coins buy specific boosters at fixed prices; nothing is sold that gives a random reward. Payment is handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their own terms and privacy policies. We never receive your payment details, name, or email. The Game only receives a confirmation of which item was purchased, used to deliver it and to restore one-time purchases through "Restore Purchases". It records the item, price, currency, and the store's transaction ID in the analytics described in section 2, and sends the item, price, and currency to Meta and TikTok as described below.

Advertising measurement (Meta SDK)

We advertise the Game on Facebook and Instagram. To measure and improve those ad campaigns, the Game includes the Meta (Facebook) SDK, which sends Meta: app installs and launches; the first time you clear a level; completing the tutorial (level 3); purchases (item, price, and currency); and the estimated revenue of ads shown in the Game. It also sends device information such as the device model, operating system, language, IP address, and on Android the advertising ID (GAID). On iOS the Meta SDK never receives the IDFA (the Game turns its advertiser tracking off), so Meta measures iOS campaigns only in aggregate. The Game has no Facebook login and never reads your Facebook account. Meta's privacy policy: facebook.com/privacy/policy.

Advertising measurement (TikTok)

We also advertise the Game on TikTok. The Game includes the TikTok App Events SDK, which sends TikTok: app installs, launches, and whether you return on the second day; the first time you clear a level; completing the tutorial (level 3); purchases (item, price, and currency); and the estimated revenue of ads shown in the Game, together with device information such as the device model, operating system, language, IP address, and on Android the advertising ID (GAID). On iOS the IDFA is shared with TikTok only if you allowed tracking in Apple's prompt; otherwise TikTok measures iOS campaigns in aggregate through Apple's SKAdNetwork. The Game turns on TikTok's Limited Data Use option. TikTok's privacy policy: tiktok.com/legal/page/us/privacy-policy/en.

5. Who we share data with

PostHog, GameAnalytics, and Google Firebase process data on our behalf to provide analytics and crash reporting. Unity LevelPlay and its ad networks receive the advertising data described in section 4, and Meta and TikTok receive the ad-measurement events described there. We do not sell your personal information for money. We may disclose information if required by law, or to a successor if the Game is transferred to another developer.

7. Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): the Game is not yet offered in these regions. Before it is, it will ask for your consent to personalized ads and ad measurement with Google's consent message, and Settings in the Game will have a "Privacy options" button to change your choice at any time; this policy will be updated then. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing before the withdrawal.
  • Tracking on iOS: Apple's tracking prompt and Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking (section 4).
  • Advertising ID on Android: reset or delete it, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device settings (section 4).
  • US state privacy laws: letting ad networks use device identifiers for interest-based ads may count as "selling" or "sharing" personal information, or "targeted advertising", under laws such as California's CCPA/CPRA. You can opt out with the device settings above or by emailing us with the subject "Do not sell or share". The Game does not collect sensitive personal information as defined by those laws, and we will not treat you differently for using your rights.

8. How long we keep data

  • Progress and settings stay on your device until you uninstall the Game. Uninstalling also deletes the anonymous install ID; a reinstall starts with a new, unrelated ID.
  • Crash reports: kept by Google for up to 90 days.
  • Analytics (PostHog, GameAnalytics): event data is kept for up to 24 months, then deleted or kept only as aggregated statistics that do not relate to any install.
  • Advertising and ad-measurement data are kept by each provider under its own policy (links in section 4).

9. International transfers

Our service providers process data in the United States and other countries where they operate, which may not offer the same protection as your country. Recipients: PostHog (United States), GameAnalytics (European Union and United States), Google Firebase (United States), Unity / ironSource, Google AdMob, Meta, and TikTok (United States and other countries). Where required, these transfers rely on the providers' Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Data is transferred over encrypted connections when the Game is in use, for the purposes and retention periods described above.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. This includes the EU and UK (GDPR), Switzerland (FADP), Brazil (LGPD), California and other US states, Canada, Japan (APPI), South Korea (PIPA), Turkey (KVKK), Australia, and India. To use these rights, email us (section 13). Because the Game has no accounts, tell us your device type, the country and approximate date you installed the Game, and the request; we may need more details to find data that relates to your install. We reply within one month (45 days for US state requests). If you disagree with our answer you can ask us to reconsider, and you can complain to your data protection authority (for example in Brazil the ANPD).

11. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher minimum age in your country, such as 16 in some EU countries), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

广告（Unity LevelPlay，广告网络为 ironSource Ads、Unity Ads、Google AdMob、Meta Audience Network）：主页底部有横幅广告，关卡之间有插屏广告，另有可自愿观看的激励视频。广告服务商会直接从设备收集：Android 的广告 ID（GAID）、iOS 的 IDFV，以及仅在你允许追踪时才有的 IDFA、设备型号、系统、语言、网络与 IP（及由此推断的大致位置），以及广告的展示、点击、完成情况，用于投放、频控、效果统计和反作弊。iOS 上游戏会在广告启动前弹一次苹果的“App 跟踪透明度”（ATT）授权：选“允许”，广告网络会拿到 IDFA，可能用于个性化广告和跨 App 的效果统计；选“要求 App 不跟踪”则拿不到。随时可以在 设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪 里更改。Android 上可能用于个性化广告，你可以在系统设置里重置或删除广告 ID、关闭广告个性化。对加州用户，游戏启用了 Meta 的“有限数据使用”（LDU）选项。iOS 上还有 ironSource Ad Quality SDK，用于检测异常、不当或欺诈广告。

广告效果衡量（TikTok）：我们也在 TikTok 投放本游戏的广告，游戏内置 TikTok App Events SDK，发送的内容与 Meta 相同：安装、启动、次日回访、首次通关、完成教学、内购、广告收入，以及设备型号、系统、语言、IP，Android 上还有广告 ID。iOS 上只有在你允许追踪时才会共享 IDFA，否则 TikTok 只能通过苹果的 SKAdNetwork 做汇总统计。游戏为 TikTok 启用了“有限数据使用”选项。

内购：可选购买“去广告”和“去广告组合包”（一次性），以及金币包（四档）、新手礼包、续关礼包。金币只能按固定价格买确定的道具，没有付费随机奖励。付款完全由 App Store / Google Play 处理，我们拿不到你的支付信息、姓名或邮箱，只收到购买了哪件商品的确认，用于发放和恢复购买；统计中记录商品、价格、币种和商店交易 ID，并把商品、价格、币种发给 Meta 和 TikTok。
