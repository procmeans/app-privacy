Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Drag to aim, release to drop. Two matching cryptids merge into the next one up. Reach each level's goal before you run out of drops, and don't let the crate overflow the line.

I bought Remove Ads but ads are still showing

Open Settings (the gear icon in the top-left corner of the map) and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you used for the purchase. If it still does not work, email us with your order number from the App Store or Google Play receipt.

What does Remove Ads include?

It permanently turns off the home-screen banner and the ads between levels on that store account. Rewarded videos (for example "+5 drops") stay available as an optional choice; they are never forced.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

Not yet. Progress is saved only on your device and is deleted when the game is uninstalled. Purchases can always be restored on the same store account.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling the game deletes everything stored on your device. The game has no accounts; the analytics it sends are anonymous. To request deletion of data connected to your install, email us and we will do what we can — see the privacy policy for what is collected.

A level seems impossible or something looks broken

Tell us the level number and what happened. Screenshots or a short screen recording help a lot.
